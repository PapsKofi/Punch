Pay shown is overtime pay only — your regular hours (up to 8/day on a weekday) are assumed to be covered by a separate salary and aren't included in these figures. On any day — weekday, weekend, or holiday — the day is anchored to a 6am start: time worked before 6am is treated as coming in early and isn't counted at all (not regular, not overtime), no matter how early you clock in (your actual clock-in time is still recorded as-is). On a weekday, hours from 6am up to the threshold are regular and the rest is overtime. On a weekend/holiday, all hours from 6am onward count as overtime. Overtime is paid at hourly rate × that day's rate multiplier. Weekend = Saturday & Sunday. Holidays are only applied to a day once marked. Adjust rates and threshold in Settings.
